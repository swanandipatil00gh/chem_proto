Nature's Recipe

The 6 Natural Ingredients

Every ingredient in EcoWash is sourced directly from nature, each serving a specific, beneficial purpose in every wash.

Natural Cleanser & Lather

Soapnuts (Reetha)

Sapindus mukorossi

Soapnuts have been used in Indian households for centuries. Their outer shells contain saponins — a natural surfactant that creates a gentle foam in water, lifting dirt and oils from fabrics naturally.

Naturally hypoallergenic, gentle on cotton, wool, and silk.
Grease Cutter & Fresh Aroma

Orange Peels

Citrus sinensis

Orange rinds are rich in D-limonene, a natural citrus solvent that breaks down stubborn oil, food stains, and grease while leaving clothes with a light, fresh, natural citrus aroma.

Repurposes food waste, replaces artificial chemical perfumes.
Stain Brightener & Softener

Lemon Peels

Citrus limon

Packed with natural citric acid, lemon peel extract helps soften hard water, dissolves mineral films on clothing, brightens whites naturally, and keeps fabrics soft without chemical softeners.

Naturally neutralizes odors and keeps clothes bright and soft.
Traditional Mineral Builder

Wood Ash

Natural Hardwood Ash Extract (K₂CO₃)

Clean hardwood ash is rich in soluble potassium carbonate. When filtered with water, it creates a mild traditional alkaline lye that helps dissolve stubborn dirt and release deep grime from cotton fibers.

Balanced with citrus acid for a gentle, skin-safe pH level.
Pure Extraction Base

Pure Water

H₂O

Pure, clean water serves as the gentle extraction medium that draws out the cleansing saponins from soapnuts, essential oils from citrus peels, and soothing herbal actives into a smooth, liquid detergent.

100% non-toxic, clean, and refreshing universal liquid carrier.
Nature's Herbal Protection

Neem Leaves

Azadirachta indica

Neem is famous for its natural protective and purifying qualities. In EcoWash, fresh neem leaves help keep the organic formulation fresh, prevent spoilage, and add natural hygienic freshness to laundry.

Replaces synthetic chemical parabens and harsh preservatives.
The Process

How EcoWash is Handcrafted

A simple, clean four-step workflow using traditional botanical extraction and careful filtration.

1

Handpick & Prepare

Soapnuts are deseeded, fresh orange and lemon peels are sliced thin, and neem leaves are washed with clean water.

2

Gentle Simmer

Ingredients are gently simmered in warm water to extract the natural foaming saponins and aromatic citrus oils.

3

Balance & Neutralize

Filtered wood ash extract is carefully combined and balanced with lemon citrus for a balanced, skin-friendly liquid.

4

Filter & Bottle

The liquid is strained through fine cloth into clean glass bottles, ready to give your laundry a gentle, natural clean.
